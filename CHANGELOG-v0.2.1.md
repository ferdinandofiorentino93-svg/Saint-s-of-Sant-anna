# Changelog · 0.2.1

- Passive: separate from manual actions and automatically resolved at their trigger windows.
- Cannoniere Spietato: the team passive triggers on every team shot while San Zorro is on the field; the Legendary version spends 3 Fatigue automatically.
- Finalizzatore Clinico (Comune): triggers automatically on a goal while San Zorro is on the field.
- Maledizione del Risparmio: its opponent debuff is removed when Manuel leaves the field or ranks up.
- Dominio Assoluto: +1 Attack is visible on each ally card and is included in the Showdown; opponent shot penalty remains applied.
- Action area simplified: available abilities are separated from blocked/not-yet-automated abilities.
- Turn guide clarifies opening hand, per-turn draw, Fatigue upkeep, bench recovery, Event timing and Break Down duration.
- Showdown comparison now includes visual Attack/Defense bars and the difference.
- Shot result displays both D20 rolls, Technique, Parry and totals.
- If the attacking side has no eligible shooter, the turn closes without getting stuck.
- CPU can complete the final defensive phase automatically.
